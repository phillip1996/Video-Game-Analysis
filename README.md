INTRODUCTION

As a Data Analyst tasked with analyzing the video game dataset, I embarked on this journey to uncover valuable insight and provide data-driven solutions to the challenges facing the gaming industry. In recent years, the video game market has witnessed exponential growth, both in terms of the number of games released and the diverse platforms they are available on.
However, with this growth comes several critical issues that demand attention and strategic decision-making.

CHALLENGES AND PROBLEMS

1.	Genre Dominance: One of the key challenges is to understanding the dominance of specific game genre. By identifying which genres have consistently performed well over the years and which one is emerging, we can help game developers make informed decisions about the type of game they should create
2.	Platform preference: The gaming landscape is vast, with games released on various platforms, including console, PC’s and mobile devices. Understanding the platforms preferences of gamers can guide marketing strategies and platform-specific game development.
3.	Regional Disparities: Sales trends vary significantly across regions, including North America, Japan, Europe, and other parts of the world. Recognizing these disparities is essential for localization and regional marketing efforts.
4.	Publisher influence: Publishers play a pivotal role in the success of games. Analyzing publisher performance can provide insight into successful publishing strategies and potential partnerships.

vi.	Regional differences in genre preference and sales pattern
vii.	What is the most sought after genre globally?
viii.	What are the Top 10 games with the highest sales?
ix.	Are there any noticeable trends or patterns in global game sales over the years?

RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on the analysis, I proposed the following recommendations:
i.	Game developers should focus on genres that shows consistent popularity while also exploring emerging trends to diversify their portfolios.
ii.	Tailor marketing strategies and game development efforts to cater to the preference of gamers on specific platforms.
iii.	Consider regional differences in game play and cultural nuance when releasing and marketing games in different regions.
iv.	Publishers can learn from successful strategies employed by top-performing publishers and consider partnerships to enhance game success.
